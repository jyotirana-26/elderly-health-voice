    Sāthī gives a rough, at-home sense of how steady your breathing sounds from day to day — it is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition, including irregular heartbeat, sleep apnea, or lung disease. Readings are estimates from your device's microphone only. If you feel unwell, or a reading concerns you, please contact a doctor rather than relying on this app. The alert button sends an email and shows saved phone numbers for you to call — it cannot dial or message anyone by itself, and in a real emergency please call your local emergency number directly. The pharmacy and delivery links open other companies' own sites or apps; Sāthī does not place orders, handle prescriptions, or process payments. All check-in data stays on this device in your browser and is not sent anywhere by this app.
